GREEN SIGNAL EC ≤ Limit
Optimal Soil Equilibrium

Salinity within biological tolerance. Moisture at field capacity (22–38%). Root osmotic potential supports unobstructed nutrient uptake.

Action Protocol:
  • Irrigation: Maintain scheduled 40-minute cycle. Do not over-water.
  • Fertilizer: Authorized for full growth-stage dose. Uptake efficiency ~95%.
  • Monitoring: Beacon pulses green every 10s. Normal observation.
AMBER SIGNAL 85% Limit / Dry
Pre-Stress Advisory

EC approaching crop threshold OR moisture near refill point (< 22%). Evaporative loss is concentrating salts around root hairs.

Action Protocol:
  • Irrigation: If dry, water immediately (+10% runtime) to dilute root salts.
  • Fertilizer: HOLD OR HALVE DOSE. Never apply full mineral salts now.
  • Inspection: Re-check console telemetry in 4–6 hours.
RED SIGNAL EC > Threshold
Critical Saline Hazard

Physiological Drought. Osmotic suction pulls water *out* of roots into soil. Root damage and yield decline underway.

Action Protocol:
  • FERTILIZER BAN: 100% halt on all chemical inputs for minimum 7 days.
  • LEACH FLUSH: Apply calculated FAO-56 extra water (+25% to +40%) in deep soak.
  • DRAINAGE: Open perimeter furrows so salt water exits field.
BLUE SIGNAL Moist > 38% Sat
Waterlogging & Hypoxia

Soil macropores choked with water. Oxygen drops below 5%, suffocating roots and triggering rapid denitrification.

Action Protocol:
  • SHUT VALVES: Stop all irrigation pumps and divert canal lines immediately.
  • RELIEF DITCHES: Rake or trench surface furrows to drain standing ponded water.
  • NO FERTILIZER: Zero fertilizer; prevents complete denitrification runoff.

FAO-56 Agronomic Formulations & Biological Models

Mathematical standards governing edge recommendations

Leaching Fraction (LR)
LR = ECw / (5×ECe - ECw)

Calculates fraction of surplus water needed to leach root zone salts.

Temp Compensation (25°C)
EC25 = EC / [1 + 0.02(T-25)]

Normalizes electrical conductivity across diurnal shifts.

Root Osmotic Potential
Ψs ≈ -0.36 × EC (bars)

Quantifies negative hydraulic suction roots must overcome to drink.
