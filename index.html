Hardware Beacon & Diagnostic Color Matrix

Field Action Protocols

Exact descriptive meanings and mandatory physical measures for farmers and extension agents when specific beacon colors show up.

Currently Triggered Signal: 🟢 GREEN SIGNAL (OPTIMAL EQUILIBRIUM)

GREEN SIGNAL

EC ≤ Safe Limit

Descriptive Meaning:

Optimal Soil Equilibrium. Salinity is well within biological tolerance. Moisture is between refill and capacity (22–38%). Roots absorb water freely with zero osmotic resistance.

Mandatory Measures to Take:

  • 1. Irrigation: Maintain scheduled standard cycle (e.g. 40-min drip/furrow). Do not over-irrigate to avoid leaching nitrogen.
  • 2. Fertilizer: Authorized for full growth-stage dose. Uptake efficiency is peak (90–95%).
  • 3. Monitoring: Beacon flashes green every 10s. No hardware or soil intervention needed.

AMBER SIGNAL

85% Threshold / Dry

Descriptive Meaning:

Pre-Stress Advisory. Salinity is within 85–100% of tolerance OR moisture has dropped near refill point (< 22%). Evapotranspiration is concentrating salts in the active root zone.

Mandatory Measures to Take:

  • 1. Irrigation: If dry, water immediately for standard runtime + 10% to dilute root zone salts and prevent salt crystallization.
  • 2. Fertilizer: DEFER OR HALVE DOSE. Never apply full mineral salts now; doing so pushes soil directly into the red critical zone.
  • 3. Inspection: Re-check reading after 4–6 hours to ensure soil water dilutes salts safely.

RED SIGNAL

EC > Crop Tolerance

Descriptive Meaning:

Critical Osmotic Shock. Salinity exceeds safe limit. "Physiological Drought" occurs—even in wet soil, osmotic pressure sucks water OUT of roots into the soil. Crop loss is underway.

Mandatory Measures to Take:

  • 1. HALT FERTILIZER: 100% ban on all chemical inputs, foliar sprays, or manures for minimum 7 days.
  • 2. LEACH FLUSH: Apply calculated FAO-56 surplus water (+25% to +40% extra volume) in deep soaks to push salts below root depth (-20cm).
  • 3. CLEAR DRAINS: Ensure perimeter furrows are open so saline flush water drains away from field.

BLUE SIGNAL

Moist > 38% Saturation

Descriptive Meaning:

Waterlogging & Hypoxia Hazard. Soil pores are completely choked with water. Oxygen drops below 5%, suffocating roots and triggering anaerobic denitrification.

Mandatory Measures to Take:

  • 1. SHUT VALVES: Cut all irrigation immediately. Divert any incoming canal flows away from plot.
  • 2. TRENCH DRAINAGE: Trench or rake relief furrows to allow ponding water to exit gravity-wise.
  • 3. NO FERTILIZER: Strictly avoid fertilizing wet soil; 100% of applied nitrogen will convert to gas or wash off.

FAO-56 Crop Tolerance Database & Biological Models

Scientific baseline calibrated to sub-Saharan Africa smallholder agricultural realities

FAO-56 Leaching Fraction (LR)

LR = ECw / (5 × ECe - ECw)

Calculates the extra depth of water needed to leach accumulated salts down past the -20cm root zone.

Standard 25°C Temp Compensation

EC25 = ECraw / [1 + 0.02 × (T - 25)]

Removes diurnal temperature distortion. Raw electrical conductivity shifts ~2% per 1°C temperature drift.

Root Osmotic Potential (Ψs)

Ψs ≈ -0.36 × EC (bars)

Quantifies the negative suction pressure plants must exert to extract soil water across root cell membranes.
